# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Expected behaviour

- Demonstrate empathy and respect.
- Give and accept constructive feedback.
- Focus discussion on reproducible technical behaviour.
- Protect other people's private information.
- Accept responsibility and repair harm when possible.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, threats, insults, or discriminatory language.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Sexualized language, imagery, or attention.
- Sustained disruption or bad-faith participation.
- Conduct reasonably considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository owner's GitHub profile. Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary.
